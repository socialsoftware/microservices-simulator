## Executive Summary

Two Critical findings share one root cause: the harness states the aggregate/event version type as
`Integer`, while `simulator/` types it `Long` in `Aggregate.version`, `Event.publisherAggregateVersion`
and `EventSubscription.subscribedVersion`. `session-d.md`'s worked ByEvent method takes
`Integer publisherVersion` and assigns it from `event.getPublisherAggregateVersion()` — code that does
not compile, and that directly contradicts `events.md` § "Always advance the cached publisher version",
which uses `Long`. This is the session-`d` path of the aggregate about to be implemented next, so it is
the finding to act on first. Beyond that, six Major findings cluster on shared-file bookkeeping under
delegated execution (`implement-aggregate-full`'s shared-file table omits files its own session sub-files
require slices to append to) and on plan-generation completeness. Check 4 (neutral domain) is clean:
578 added harness lines scanned, zero matches.
