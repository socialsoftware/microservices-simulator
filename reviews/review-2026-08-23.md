Three Critical findings, all in the event-wiring path. `docs/concepts/events.md` and
`session-d.md` state that `EventApplicationService.handleSubscribedEvent()` does not call
`subscribesEvent()`; the framework does call it, and `rule-enforcement-patterns.md` § P2 and

`service.md` § Copy-on-Write Rule forbids unconditionally. Path validity is clean and the
neutral-domain check is clean (0 hits over 372 added lines). Fix the three Critical items before
the next aggregate begins; the event-wiring session is the one that consumes all of them.
